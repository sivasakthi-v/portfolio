Magic To Do

Organize and track your to-dos without leaving Figma.

Open in Figma ↗

Magic To Do lets you organize and track your to-dos without leaving Figma — so your task list lives right where the design work happens.

Features
  • Tasks tab (live): create & rename folders, add & remove tasks
  • Check tasks off into Completed, restore with one click
  • Everything saved between sessions
  • Magic Tasks (coming soon): paste requirements, AI spins up a folder of actionable tasks

Flow

Your calm, private finance planner for everyday life.

Visit Flow ↗

Flow is a local-first personal finance planner that helps you capture expenses, organize accounts, plan future spending, and manage debts with clarity — all in one simple, secure, thoughtfully designed space. Instead of overwhelming dashboards, it helps you understand where your money is today and confidently plan where it goes tomorrow.

Features
  • Capture expenses and organize accounts
  • Plan future spending with clarity
  • Manage debts in one place
  • Local-first — private and secure by design
