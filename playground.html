Magic To Do

Organize and track your to-dos without leaving Figma.

Open in Figma

A small Figma plugin that keeps your to-do list right on the canvas, so your tasks live where the design work already happens.

Features
  • Tasks tab (live): create and rename folders, add and remove tasks
  • Check tasks off into Completed, restore with one click
  • Everything saved between sessions
  • Magic Tasks (coming soon): paste requirements, AI spins up a folder of tasks

Features
