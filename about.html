The Story

Curious, slightly chaotic, and living in my own movie.

ChatGPT once roasted me as a "multi-dimensional, self-confessed doodler and overthinker extraordinaire," and honestly, I doubt anyone could top that. I'm M24, a desi guy, single — and perpetually mid-thought about something.
