The Story

Systems first, then the canvas.

Over the last 3+ years I've worked across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, travel, and AI products, contributing to everything from user flows and interfaces to documentation, design systems, and product thinking.

I believe the most powerful thing a designer can do is match UX vocabulary with real-world user mental models. So I try to understand the systems, the mental models, and the real data before I ever touch the canvas. Away from the screen, I'm usually chasing surrealism in graphic design and following a good rabbit hole about cinema or the cosmos.

I've also vibe-coded a few things of my own: a Figma to-do plugin (live), a local personal journal, and a productivity app. All small. All mine. All satisfying.
