The Brief

Where AI and communications expertise become one.

Comms.AI is a professional-grade platform for PR practitioners, in-house communications teams, and startup founders managing their own narrative. It combines structured guidance with AI generation across every major communications deliverable — from press releases and key messages to social strategy, narrative frameworks, and crisis communications.

AI tools often feel like magic boxes: everything in, or nothing out. Comms.AI was built to be the opposite — a transparent, structured, expert-guided process where AI is a collaborator, not a replacement for thinking. The core move is sequencing: users answer a structured questionnaire first, and that organised input is what makes the generation genuinely good.
