Executive Summary

One strategic input, fanned out into every asset a comms team ships.

Comms.Ai is an AI communications suite for PR and corporate-communications teams. It started life as PRGPT, a single press-release generator, and grew across eight documented design versions into a seven-module platform. Feed it one strategic input, a brief, a narrative, a message house, and it fans out into press releases, social strategies and posts, articles, PR campaign playbooks, and storyboards.

Its differentiator is Dynamic Intelligence, a real-time PR consultant layer that interrogates a brief for gaps before the AI writes anything. Everything I designed sits on one behavioural grammar: structured briefs replace blank prompts, generation reuses one source document everywhere, and first-class versioning keeps iteration safe.

The Problem

Most of the effort in PR is structured information gathering, not writing.

A credible press release takes senior comms people hours, and agencies days. Generic AI chat tools do not fix that, they make it worse, because they never ask what is missing.

Problem 01

Fluent but not credible

Generic AI produces confident output that never asks what is missing, so quotes, proof points, and context get invented or quietly dropped.

Problem 02

PR is never one asset

The same announcement becomes a release, a social calendar, posts, articles, and a campaign plan, and teams re-brief every tool separately.

Problem 03

Iteration without discipline

Drafts go through many rounds. Without version control, teams lose track of which edit said what.

Problem 04

The missing consultant

The person who would normally ask the sharp questions before writing, the senior strategist, is exactly who most teams cannot spare.

Design Language

The interface recedes so the output reads like the real artefact.

A dark, focused workspace with a magenta-to-orange gradient identity, monospaced module headers, and a document-white canvas where generated content appears.

01

Focused dark workspace

The tool stays quiet and out of the way. Attention goes to the brief and the draft, not the chrome.

02

Document-white canvas

Generated content lands on a paper-like surface, so a release reads like a release, not a chat bubble.

03

One behavioural grammar

Empty states, loaders, a five-step tutorial, rename, discard, save-as-draft, and success toasts are specified for every module, so the whole suite behaves the same way.

The Product

Seven modules. One source of truth.

Each module solves a distinct comms need. What ties them together is that any one output can become the seed for the next.

Module 01

Press Release

The flagship. A three-step guided journey, Brief, Draft, Final, with a structured questionnaire, immutable original, refine-into-new-versions, generate-titles, and side-by-side compare before finalising.

Module 02

Narratives

A four-step storytelling flow, Context, Strategy, Vision, Final Draft, pairing an open canvas with DI-suggested questions about the company, audience, and market.

Module 03

Message House

The classic strategic-comms artefact: one key-message roof over three supporting messages, each with proof points, ready to seed every other asset.

Module 04

Social Strategy

From a source document, a strategic calendar of themes, platform mix, copy, hashtags, and visual notes, viewable as a table or calendar, expandable into platform-ready posts.

Module 05

Articles

Theme suggestions from the source, DI enrichment, then a full thought-leadership article, itself chainable into social posts.

Module 06

PR Plan

An executive-summary-led campaign playbook: vision, key pillars of impact, and the signature moments that carry a campaign.

Module 07

Storyboard

A narrative storybook treatment of the campaign, built for creative teams to run with.

The engine underneath

One source document, a release, a message house, a narrative, becomes the input to social posts, articles, campaigns, and storyboards. Write the strategy once, and every downstream asset inherits it.

The product's soul
Dynamic Intelligence

A real-time consultant that reads your brief before the AI does.

Dynamic Intelligence is real-time PR consulting. It analyses a brief, identifies the critical gaps and content opportunities, then delivers research, strategic context, and recommended answers, before a single word of the release is generated.

After a brief, DI generates targeted questions. Each one comes with research-backed context and a recommended answer the user can accept or overwrite. That enhanced brief is what flows into generation, which is exactly why the output holds up.

DI is also where the product earns its keep. Usage draws on credits, tracked through a transaction summary and per-release credit views. I designed that economy so the cost reads as value delivered, not a paywall in the way.

Step 01

Analyse the brief

DI reads the structured brief and surfaces the gaps a senior strategist would catch, the missing quote, the unproven claim, the absent context.

Step 02

Ask, with a recommendation

Each gap becomes a question paired with research and a suggested answer. Accept it, edit it, or replace it. The user stays in control.

Step 03

Feed the enhanced brief

The richer brief flows into generation, and the credit spend is accounted for transparently, so the value is visible.

Role, Process & Collaboration

Designed end to end, as the sole designer.

I owned Comms.Ai from research framing and IA through wireframes across every version, high-fidelity dark-theme UI, component systems, and microcopy, including DI's product definition and the tutorial voice. A senior designer gave light mentorship, while direction was pressure-tested in recurring reviews with the product manager and client.

Each review round is traceable as a version section in the wireframe file, right down to a dated December-2025 update that added media-pitch generation to narratives and releases.
