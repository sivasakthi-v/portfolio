Overview

One ecosystem, many surfaces, held to one standard.

KBZ is one of Myanmar's largest financial institutions. The work spanned two worlds under a single design language: the consumer-facing KBZ Pay Market, an 8M+ user commerce platform, and the KBZ Bank core-banking MiniApp suite, where regulated banking workflows meet everyday mobile expectations.

The challenge across both was the same: take dense, compliance-heavy processes like identity verification, approvals, and account configuration, and make them feel as clear and quick as any modern app, without ever weakening the controls a bank depends on. The flagship, Video Banking, front-loads KYC before the queue: customers finish document upload and a live selfie check up front, while a priority-aware queue and agent-assignment flows keep operations moving. It launched June 2026.
