Overview

One ecosystem, many surfaces — held to one standard.

KBZ is one of Myanmar's largest financial institutions. The work spanned two worlds under a single design language: the consumer-facing KBZ Pay Market — an 8M+ user commerce platform — and the KBZ Bank core-banking MiniApp suite, where regulated banking workflows meet everyday mobile expectations.

The challenge across both: take dense, compliance-heavy processes — identity verification, approvals, account configuration — and make them feel as clear and quick as any modern app, without ever weakening the controls a bank depends on. The flagship, Video Banking, front-loads KYC before the queue: customers complete document upload and live selfie verification up front, while a priority-aware queue and agent-assignment flows keep operations moving. It launched June 2026.
